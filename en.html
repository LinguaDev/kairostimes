Man fancies himself God.
And the machines whisper that he's right.

Artificial intelligence, biotechnology, and robotics offer us power, longevity, and control. But as the world accelerates, your soul is left with unanswered questions.

Why live if machines do everything? What if the meaning of your life isn't in what you produce, but in Who created you?

Here you won't find algorithms that understand you, but a truth that transforms you. A perspective that science cannot give you. An anchor when progress overwhelms you.

Juan Pineda August 17, 2026
Explore a new perspective

The modern man's dilemma:
Progress or spiritual abyss?

We explore the viability of believing in a Creator when technology allows us to extend life, create virtual worlds, and dominate nature. A look from Ecclesiastes to transhumanism.

When man plays at being God

Genetic engineering, AI, and robotics give us unprecedented power. But the human heart remains empty. Were we created for something more than just efficiency and long life?

“Vanity of vanities, all is vanity” — Ecclesiastes 1:2

Technological depression: the price of progress

More connectivity leads to more loneliness. More automation leads to more unemployment and identity crisis. The digital world gives us answers, but takes away the fundamental question: Why do I exist?

“What does it profit a man to gain the whole world, if he loses his soul?” — Matthew 16:26

Faith as philosophical resistance

Believing in God in the 21st century is not an act of ignorance, but of profound lucidity. Recognizing that creation has a Creator and that morality transcends algorithms is the only anchor to avoid shipwreck in technological relativism.

Are you ready to explore the truth? Science answers the "how," but only God answers the "why."

The Gospel: True hope for the technological man

No matter how advanced technology becomes, the problem of sin and death remains the great challenge of humanity. No algorithm, no implant, no artificial intelligence can save your soul.

1

God loves you and has a purpose

“For God so loved the world that He gave His only begotten Son” (John 3:16).

2

Sin separates us from God

“For all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God” (Romans 3:23).

3

Christ paid the price on the cross

“But God demonstrates His own love toward us, in that while we were still sinners, Christ died for us” (Romans 5:8).

4

Resurrection and eternal life

“If you confess with your mouth the Lord Jesus and believe in your heart that God has raised Him from the dead, you will be saved” (Romans 10:9).

Today is the day of salvation. Whether you are rich or poor, a technician or a philosopher, Jesus is calling you. Let your life find eternal meaning in Him.
